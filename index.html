What is Net Change (Weekly)?
The week-over-week shift in Non-Commercial Net Position — how much big money added or removed from their long or short lean between the two most recent CFTC Tuesday reports.
Formula:
This week's (Longs − Shorts)
  minus Last week's (Longs − Shorts)

How is Momentum derived?
Momentum compares the bias direction to the weekly change direction — is big money pressing their current position, or backing off?
LONG bias:
 Wk change positive → BUILDING ↑ (adding to long lean)
 Wk change negative → COOLING ↓ (trimming long lean)

SHORT bias:
 Wk change negative → BUILDING ↓ (adding to short lean)
 Wk change positive → COOLING ↑ (covering shorts)

NEUTRAL bias: → MIXED

COOLING doesn't mean the bias reverses — it means conviction is softening. Watch for COOLING + consecutive red bars together as an early warning.
